Why MSC-Seeded Hydrogels Behave Differently from Mature Chondrocyte Constructs: Undifferentiated bone-marrow MSCs exhibit up to \(2\times\) higher baseline oxygen consumption (\(V_{\max} \approx 37.4\text{ fmol/h/cell}\)) than differentiated chondrocytes (\(18.7\text{ fmol/h/cell}\)) and require a lineage-commitment signal. In TGF-\(\beta 3\) media, local physiological hypoxia (\(\sim 2\text{–}12\%\text{ O}_2\)) stabilizes HIF-1\(\alpha\), accelerating MSC differentiation into chondrocytes and upregulating aggrecan/sGAG synthesis in the construct core while lowering metabolic \(V_{\max}\) toward the chondrocytic rate. However, where local \(\text{O}_2\) falls below the critical survival threshold (\(<1\%\text{ O}_2\)), nutrient/oxygen starvation triggers apoptosis (DNA loss) and halts matrix deposition.

1. Clinical & Bioengineering Motivation: Scaling Up Cartilage Grafts

Bone marrow-derived mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) encapsulated in 3D hydrogels (such as agarose) are a promising cell source for articular cartilage repair. While most laboratory studies characterize small cylindrical constructs (\(\varnothing 4\text{–}5\text{ mm}\)), clinical defects require scaling up construct thickness and diameter. Buckley, Meyer, & Kelly (2012) investigated how construct geometry (\(\varnothing 4\times 1.5\text{ mm}\), \(\varnothing 5\times 3.0\text{ mm}\), \(\varnothing 6\times 4.5\text{ mm}\), and \(\varnothing 8\times 4.5\text{ mm}\)) and external oxygen tension (\(20\%\text{ O}_2\) normoxia vs. \(5\%\text{ O}_2\) hypoxia) govern internal oxygen gradients, spatial extracellular matrix (ECM) architecture, and compressive mechanical properties over 42 days of chondrogenic culture.

2. Nonlinear Michaelis–Menten Reaction–Diffusion PDE (Eqs. 1–3)

Oxygen transport within a cylindrical cell-seeded hydrogel of radius \(R = \varnothing/2\) and height \(H\) is governed by the mass-conservation reaction–diffusion partial differential equation (Eq. 1):

\[\frac{\partial C_{\text{ox}}}{\partial t} = \nabla \cdot \left(D_{\text{construct}} \nabla C_{\text{ox}}\right) - n_{\text{cell}}\,\frac{V_{\max}\,C_{\text{ox}}}{K_m + C_{\text{ox}}}\]

In axisymmetric cylindrical coordinates \((r, z)\) at quasi-steady state (\(\partial C_{\text{ox}}/\partial t = 0\)):

\[D_{\text{construct}}\left(\frac{\partial^2 C_{\text{ox}}}{\partial r^2} + \frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial C_{\text{ox}}}{\partial r} + \frac{\partial^2 C_{\text{ox}}}{\partial z^2}\right) = n_{\text{cell}}\,\frac{V_{\max}\,C_{\text{ox}}}{K_m + C_{\text{ox}}}\]

The effective diffusivity \(D_{\text{construct}}\) through the agarose hydrogel polymer network of volume fraction \(\phi_f\) (e.g., \(\phi_f = 0.02\) for \(2\%\text{ w/v}\) agarose) is given by the Mackie and Meares (1955) obstruction relation (Eq. 2):

\[D_{\text{construct}} = D_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}\,\frac{(1 - \phi_f)^2}{(1 + \phi_f)^2}\]

For oxygen in culture medium at \(37^\circ\text{C}\), \(D_{\text{H}_2\text{O}} = 3.0 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}\), yielding \(D_{\text{construct}} = 2.77 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}\) in \(2\%\) agarose. Cellular uptake follows Michaelis–Menten kinetics with \(K_m = 6.3 \times 10^{-3}\text{ mol/m}^3\) (\(\approx 0.64\%\text{ O}_2\)) and maximum consumption rate \(V_{\max} = 18.7\text{ fmol/h/cell}\) (\(5.19 \times 10^{-18}\text{ mol/s/cell}\)) for differentiated chondrocytes, or up to \(2\times V_{\max} = 37.4\text{ fmol/h/cell}\) for undifferentiated MSCs (Eq. 3).

3. Boundary Conditions & The Impermeable Culture Dish Asymmetry (Figs. 1–3)

  • Top Surface (\(z = H\)) & Lateral Radial Wall (\(r = R\)): Exposed to well-mixed culture medium at Dirichlet concentration \(C_{\text{ox}} = C_{\text{ext}}\) (where \(20\%\text{ O}_2 \equiv 0.197\text{ mol/m}^3\) and \(5\%\text{ O}_2 \equiv 0.0493\text{ mol/m}^3\) by Henry's law at \(37^\circ\text{C}\)).
  • Central Symmetry Axis (\(r = 0\)): Zero radial flux \(\left.\frac{\partial C_{\text{ox}}}{\partial r}\right|_{r=0} = 0\).
  • Bottom Culture Dish Surface (\(z = 0\)): Resting flat against impermeable tissue culture plastic, enforcing a zero-flux Neumann boundary condition \(\left.\frac{\partial C_{\text{ox}}}{\partial z}\right|_{z=0} = 0\).

Because oxygen cannot enter through the bottom surface (\(z=0\)), the axial profile is asymmetric and the global minimum oxygen concentration always occurs at the Bottom Core \((r = 0, z = 0)\).

4. Opposing Spatial Gradients of sGAG and Collagen (Figs. 4–6 & Table 1)

By coring and bisecting constructs into concentric subregions—Top Annulus (TA), Bottom Annulus (BA), Top Core (TC), and Bottom Core (BC) (plus Top/Bottom Inner Annulus TIA/BIA for \(\varnothing 8\times 4.5\text{ mm}\) constructs, Fig. 6D)—Buckley, Meyer, & Kelly (2012) discovered a striking divergence in matrix synthesis at \(20\%\text{ O}_2\):

  • sGAG Concentrates in the Core (TC & BC): In \(\varnothing 8\times 4.5\text{ mm}\) constructs at \(20\%\text{ O}_2\), sulfated glycosaminoglycan (Alcian Blue) is significantly higher in the core (\(\text{TC } 1.18 \pm 0.06\%\text{ w/w}\), \(\text{BC } 1.19 \pm 0.01\%\text{ w/w}\)) than the outer annulus (\(\text{TA } 0.62 \pm 0.01\%\text{ w/w}\), \(\text{BA } 0.88 \pm 0.11\%\text{ w/w}\)). Self-induced core hypoxia (\(\sim 10.8\%\text{ O}_2\) at \(1\times V_{\max}\), \(\sim 2.6\%\text{ O}_2\) at \(2\times V_{\max}\)) promotes HIF-1\(\alpha\)/SOX9-driven chondrogenesis and proteoglycan synthesis.
  • Collagen Concentrates in the Periphery (TA & BA): Total collagen (Picrosirius Red / hydroxyproline) in \(\varnothing 8\times 4.5\text{ mm}\) constructs is dramatically higher in the normoxic annulus (\(\text{TA } 2.79 \pm 0.74\%\text{ w/w}\), \(\text{BA } 2.89 \pm 0.71\%\text{ w/w}\)) than the inner annulus (\(\text{TIA } 0.71 \pm 0.23\%\), \(\text{BIA } 0.44 \pm 0.10\%\)) and core (\(\text{TC } 0.74 \pm 0.13\%\text{ w/w}\), \(\text{BC } 0.46 \pm 0.18\%\text{ w/w}\)), reflecting the molecular \(\text{O}_2\) dependence of enzymatic collagen hydroxylation and cross-linking.

6. Biomechanical Structure–Function Relationships (Fig. 7A–G)

Unconfined compression testing (10% ramp-and-hold stress relaxation for \(E_{\text{eq}}\), followed by 1% cyclic strain at \(1\text{ Hz}\) for \(E_{\text{dyn}}\)) demonstrated that bulk compressive stiffness correlates positively with both total sGAG (\(r^2 = 0.61\text{–}0.65\)) and total collagen content (\(r^2 = 0.65\text{–}0.72\)). At \(20\%\text{ O}_2\), scaling up from \(\varnothing 4\times 1.5\text{ mm}\) to \(\varnothing 8\times 4.5\text{ mm}\) lowers internal core oxygen (\(18.2\% \to 10.8\%\)), which stimulates MSC chondrogenesis and increases whole-construct sGAG (\(0.14\% \to 0.88\%\text{ w/w}\)), collagen (\(0.31\% \to 1.62\%\text{ w/w}\)), \(E_{\text{eq}}\) (\(23.5 \to 36.52\text{ kPa}\)), and \(E_{\text{dyn}}\) (\(90.81 \to 327.51\text{ kPa}\)). Culturing \(\varnothing 5\times 3.0\text{ mm}\) constructs at \(5\%\text{ O}_2\) (\(\text{Core O}_2 = 1.9\%\)) produces a 1.9-fold increase in \(E_{\text{dyn}}\) (\(163.0 \to 310.0\text{ kPa}\)) and boosts \(E_{\text{eq}}\) (\(23.2 \to 33.0\text{ kPa}\)), whereas scaling to \(\varnothing 6\times 4.5\text{ mm}\) at \(5\%\text{ O}_2\) (\(\text{Core O}_2 = 0.7\%\)) reduces viable DNA (\(62.50 \to 51.80\text{ ng/mg}\)) and suppresses collagen accumulation (\(2.22\% \to 0.69\%\text{ w/w}\)).
